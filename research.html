Developing low-power, high-reliability embedded system architectures for real-time applications and edge computing environments.

Exploring Holographic Multiple-Input Multiple-Output (HMIMO) surface architectures to optimize wireless communications and energy propagation.

Architecting fault-tolerant computing frameworks capable of maintaining system integrity and accuracy under physical and soft errors.
