Lab Director

Abhishek Moitra

Assistant Professor · Washington State University

Research interests
Hardware and algorithm co-design, neuromorphic computing, compute-in-memory, and large language model optimization
Bio

Abhishek Moitra earned his B.E. at BITS Pilani’s Goa campus and completed his M.S. and Ph.D. at Yale University. He joined Washington State University’s School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science as an assistant professor in 2025. He is a member of IEEE.

His research combines hardware and algorithm design to improve the performance and energy efficiency of machine learning systems, with an emphasis on neuromorphic computing. His work has appeared at MLSys and DAC and in IEEE TCAD and Applied Physics Reviews. He received the 2025 IEEE TCAD Donald O. Pederson Best Paper Award and the 2024 Bell Labs Outstanding Innovation Award.
