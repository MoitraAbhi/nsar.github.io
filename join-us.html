Join Our Lab

We are actively looking for highly motivated PhD students, Master's researchers, and Undergraduate research assistants to join our team at Washington State University (WSU). Our research focuses on Computer Architecture, hardware-software algorithm codesign, embedded systems, HMIMO surfaces, and reliable computing architectures.

Open Positions

Ph.D. Position

Ph.D. Research Assistantships (Fully Funded)

We have open fully-funded Ph.D. positions (Research Assistantships) for Fall and Spring semesters. Candidates should have a strong background in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or Electrical Engineering.

Preferred Qualifications:
  • Strong C/C++ and Python programming skills.
  • Background in Computer Architecture, VLSI, Embedded Systems, or Machine Learning hardware.
  • Prior research experience or high academic standing (GPA > 3.2).
M.S. & Undergrad

Undergraduate & Master's Researchers

WSU undergraduate students and Master's students interested in hardware/software codesign, system prototyping, or experimental benchmarking are welcome to join for independent research credits (CPT_S 499 / 595) or research assistantships.

How to Apply

If you are interested in joining the lab, please send an email directly to Abhishek Moitra with the following details:

Email Subject Line Format: [Prospective Student] Your Name - Degree (e.g., [Prospective Student] Guillermo Astillero - PhD Fall 2027)
Please attach the following documents in PDF format:
  • Curriculum Vitae (CV) detailing your academic background, skills, projects, and publication list (if any).
  • Unofficial Transcripts (Undergraduate / Graduate).
  • Brief Statement of Interest (1-2 paragraphs in the email body explaining why you want to join our lab and how your background aligns with our research areas).
Note for Prospective PhD Applicants: You must also submit a formal application through the WSU Graduate School Portal selecting the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS). Mention Abhishek Moitra in your Statement of Purpose.
