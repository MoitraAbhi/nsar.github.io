Nanoscale Systems Architecture Research Lab

About Us

The NSAR Lab at Washington State University develops energy-efficient, scalable computing systems through device–architecture–algorithm co-optimization. We connect the physical capabilities of emerging devices with hardware architectures and computational methods, jointly optimizing computation, memory, sensing, and communication.

Our research spans neuromorphic and in-sensor computing, heterogeneous chiplet systems, and intelligent edge platforms, translating nanoscale device advances into improvements in system performance, adaptability, and energy efficiency.
